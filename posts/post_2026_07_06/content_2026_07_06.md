

Tiago Ferreira was invited to participate in the [“Quantum-SOPHA and Armchairs” workshop](https://sites.google.com/view/quantum-sopha/news-and-events), held from 29 June to 3 July 2026 in Peyresq, a village in the French Alps.

The workshop marked the conclusion of the five-year Quantum-SOPHA ANR project, which focused on one-dimensional quantum many-body systems and their equilibrium and nonequilibrium dynamics. The event provided an overview of the consortium’s scientific achievements while bringing together consortium members and external researchers to discuss emerging challenges, exchange new ideas, and lay the foundations for future collaborations.

During the workshop, Tiago Ferreira delivered a presentation entitled “Light as a Versatile Platform for Analogue Physical Simulations and Quantum Imaging Applications.” The presentation highlighted the QUANTOS team’s latest results and advances in analogue quantum simulation. It also introduced the team’s recent research in quantum imaging using single photons and undetected photons. <a href="../../posts/post_2026_07_06/tiago_presentation_at_Peyresq4.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Click here to see the presentation. </a>

Tiago Ferreira thanks the workshop organizers for the invitation and for the opportunity to present and discuss the research being conducted by the QUANTOS team.
